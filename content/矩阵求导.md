- 链式法则公式
    - z经过y对x求导
        - $$\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial y^\intercal}{\partial x} \cdot \frac{\partial z}{\partial y}$$
            - 形状
                - $$\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}$$形状为x
                - $$\frac{\partial y^{\mathsf{T}}}{\partial x}$$形状为xy
                - $$\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}$$形状为y
    - z对多级x求导
        - $$\frac{\partial z}{\partial x_1} = (\frac{\partial x_n}{\partial x_{n-1}^\intercal} \frac{\partial x_{n-1}}{\partial x_{n-2}^\intercal }... \frac{\partial x_2}{\partial x_{1}^\intercal})^\intercal \frac{\partial z}{\partial x_n}$$
- 常用公式
    - $$z = f(y) \\ y = wx$$
        - $$\frac{\partial z}{\partial w} = \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} x^\intercal$$
