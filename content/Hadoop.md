    - 算

    - [[MapReduce]]-> [[MapReduce]]

    - 2003-2004: Google公开部分GFS和MapReduce
    - 2005: Hadoop成为Apache Lucene子项目Nutch了一部分
    - 2006.3: MapReduce和NDFS(Nutch Distributed File System)纳入Hadoop
